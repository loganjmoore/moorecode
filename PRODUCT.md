# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

MooreCode serves people evaluating Logan Moore's iOS and web projects or consulting work, plus developers and hands-on makers looking for practical project notes and 3D-printing guidance.

## Product Purpose

MooreCode is Logan Moore's personal website for his apps, consulting work, and hands-on projects. It helps visitors explore the portfolio, read practical guides, and contact Logan about relevant work.

## Positioning

The site connects a solo developer's shipped software and consulting practice with practical notes drawn from making and testing real projects.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse a static website, compare projects and guides, use practical resources, and can continue to the site's existing contact workflow. The site is deployed through GitHub Pages.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with no application framework or build dependency.
- Search resources must expose crawlable HTML, canonical URLs, sitemap discovery, accessible native controls, and transparent assumptions.
- Existing consented PostHog analytics may be reused. New trackers, external embeds, dependencies, and unsupported product claims are out of scope.
- Cost estimates are planning aids, not supplier quotes.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is MooreCode. The site uses direct, practical language and describes Logan as a solo developer, maker, and lifelong tinkerer. New surfaces should preserve the incumbent dark interface, warm accent palette, typography, navigation, and footer patterns documented in `DESIGN.md`.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains public project pages, consulting copy, twelve blog articles, a sitemap, an RSS feed, PostHog consent handling, and SEO contract tests. It does not contain verified customer savings, vendor pricing, conversion outcomes, or a deployed calculator result, so future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Prefer useful, inspectable tools and concrete guidance over marketing claims.
- Keep source assumptions visible and editable.
- Preserve visitor privacy and the site's existing consent boundary.
- Make every public resource discoverable and usable without special software.
- Treat estimates as starting points that users verify against their own workflow and quotes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Interactive resources must use labeled native controls, visible focus, keyboard operation, responsive layouts, and status output that assistive technology can announce.
