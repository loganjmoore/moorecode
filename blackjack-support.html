Frequently asked questions

Is this real-money gambling?

No. Blackjack Now is for entertainment only. You play with free virtual chips. There is no real-money wagering, and there is no way to win real money or prizes.

I ran out of chips. How do I get more?

Open the Settings tab and tap Reset Bankroll. This restores your chips to your starting bankroll without changing your stats. You can also adjust your starting bankroll amount in Settings before resetting.

Can I change the table rules?

Yes. In Settings you can toggle dealer hits soft 17, blackjack pays 3:2 vs 6:5, surrender, double-after-split, the number of decks, and your starting bankroll.

How do I turn off sound or haptics?

Open Settings and use the Sound and Haptics toggles. Sound also respects your device's mute switch.

How do I practice strategy?

Use the Trainer tab to drill basic-strategy decisions, and the Strategy tab for a full basic-strategy chart based on the current rules.

How do ads work?

The game shows occasional ads through Google AdMob, and offers an optional "Tip the Dealer" rewarded ad. On first launch you'll be asked whether to allow tracking; the game works the same whether you allow it or not. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Blackjack Now does not require an account and stores your game progress only on your device. Deleting the app removes all local game data.
