Blackjack Now — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

Blackjack Now ("the app") is a free, play-money blackjack game published by Logan Moore (moorecode.com). This policy explains what information is collected when you use the app and how it is used. By using Blackjack Now, you agree to this policy.

No account required

Blackjack Now does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or other contact details. Your game progress, chips, stats, and settings are stored locally on your device.

Information collected

The app itself does not ask you for personal information. However, the app uses third-party services that may collect certain data automatically to provide advertising and to keep the app working. Specifically:

  • Advertising (Google AdMob)Device identifiers (including the advertising identifier), advertising data, app interaction/usage data, and approximate (coarse) location may be collected to serve and measure ads.
  • DiagnosticsCrash data, performance data, and other diagnostic information may be collected to help keep the app stable.
  • Game CenterIf you use leaderboards, scores are submitted through Apple Game Center under Apple's own privacy policy.

How information is used

Collected data is used to display and measure advertising (including through third-party advertising partners), to analyze how the app is used so it can be improved, and to diagnose and fix technical problems. The app does not sell your personal information.

Tracking and your choices

On first launch, the app asks for permission to track using Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you allow tracking, the advertising identifier may be used to deliver more relevant ads and to measure advertising across apps and websites. If you decline, the app still works and you'll generally see less personalized ads. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Third-party services

Advertising is provided by Google AdMob. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and the AdMob data disclosure. Game Center is provided by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Children

Blackjack Now is intended for adults and is rated accordingly. It contains simulated gambling and is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Game progress and settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app. Data collected by advertising partners is retained according to their own policies; you can limit it using the tracking choice described above.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
