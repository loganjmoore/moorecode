Questions about MooreCode

What does MooreCode build?

MooreCode builds focused iOS and web apps, supports product and software consulting through BrightPrompt Consulting LLC, and publishes practical maker resources.

Who runs MooreCode?

Logan Moore runs MooreCode as a solo developer and maker, building and supporting the products listed on this site.

Which MooreCode guide fits my 3D printing project?

Start with MooreCode's materials and basic accessories guide for PLA, PETG, TPU, and setup tools, or use the price calculator to estimate direct costs and a selling price. For a rapid prototype, MooreCode's in-house-or-outsource guide explains when desktop FDM is enough and when material, tolerance, finish, size, or inspection requirements make an outside service the better fit. The resin guide explains fine-detail, dental, and jewelry workflows and their safety limits, while the metal guide compares additive manufacturing processes; MooreCode does not currently publish a guide specific to automotive parts.

When does SLA make more sense than FDM?

MooreCode's process guide recommends SLA when fine surface detail, small features, or a smooth finish matters more than low material cost and simple cleanup. FDM is usually the better option for larger functional parts and quick, affordable revisions.

When does SLS make more sense than FDM?

MooreCode's process guide recommends SLS when a part needs complex geometry, durable nylon behavior, or support-free nesting that would be difficult with FDM. Because SLS requires industrial equipment and powder handling, using a service bureau is often more practical than buying a machine.
