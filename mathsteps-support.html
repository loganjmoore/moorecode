MathSteps: Staircase Sprint — Support

Answer. Jump. Reach the light.

MathSteps is an arithmetic staircase game. Answer addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division questions to climb themed staircases in short timed rounds.

Development preview. This app is being prepared for its first App Store release.

Help with the app

How do I start?

Choose the first unlocked staircase. Answer the arithmetic questions to move up the stairs.

How do I unlock another staircase?

Clear the current summit to open the next challenge. Later stages introduce additional operations and climbing rules.

What happens after a wrong answer?

A wrong answer can cost time or momentum. Try the round again and work toward the summit.

How do I report a problem?

Email the app and iOS versions, the staircase name, and what happened. If an answer seems wrong, include the full question and the answer shown.
