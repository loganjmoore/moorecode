Southside Explorer — Support

Explore an interactive Christmas world.

Southside Explorer is an interactive 3D walkthrough of the Southside Christmas scene. Move through themed lands, look around, and explore the layout from a visitor’s perspective.

Development preview. This app is being prepared for its first App Store release.

Help with the app

How do I move?

Use the on-screen movement and turning controls. The Go to menu selects a location in the scene.

Can I see the whole layout?

Use Aerial view when available to look over the scene, then return to the walkthrough.

Is this a booking or ticketing app?

No. This app is a 3D walkthrough. It does not sell attraction tickets, take reservations, or announce operating dates.

How do I report a rendering problem?

Email the app and iOS versions, your device model, and the land or view where the issue occurs.
