Billing & Invoicing Software Pricing for Service Businesses (2026)

Billing software pricing is the subscription and related cost of creating, sending, tracking, and collecting invoices. In this five-tool service-business sample, advertised subscriptions run from free for Zoho Invoice to $499 a month for Jobber's highest publicly listed five-user plan. This is not a market-wide price range. Total cost depends on the subscription model, users, invoice or client caps, payment-processing fees, and any setup or migration charges.

For a service business sending 60 invoices a month, the lowest verified subscription that clearly fits two logins in this sample is Billdu Standard at $119.99 a year. Invoice Simple Premium also fits the invoice volume at $219.99 a year, but its public pricing page does not state an included-user limit. Tofu Team is $348 a year. Zoho's current public limit details and a two-person Jobber Core price were not stated clearly enough to call either one a verified fit.

Assessment method and date: I checked all five publishers' primary pricing or help pages on November 17, 2026. The comparison uses invoice or client limits, included users, billing interval, trial terms, disclosed payment fees, and the cost of a two-person example sending 60 invoices a month. Temporary promotional prices are excluded. “Not publicly stated” means I could not confirm the fact on the publisher's current public pages. The example is an illustrative calculation, not a quote.

Which invoicing apps have a free plan?

Zoho Invoice is the only complete free subscription I could confirm from a publisher's current pages. Its public pages say usage limits apply and can change, but did not expose enough current detail to verify the 60-invoice, two-user example. Invoice Simple lets a desktop user create two free invoices before choosing a plan; that is a try-before-subscription allowance, not a verified ongoing free tier. Tofu and Billdu list 7-day trials, while Jobber lists a 14-day trial.

Is Jobber a simple invoicing app?

Jobber is field service software that also sends invoices. Its plans include work before the invoice, such as quotes, jobs, and scheduling. That makes a direct price-only comparison incomplete: a shop that needs those workflows is buying more than invoice generation. I also build an invoicing app, WaffleInvoice, which is on my projects page. It is not in this comparison, and I did not price it here, so weigh my interest accordingly.

What affects billing software pricing?

Five things change the real cost beyond the headline subscription:

  1. Usage caps: Invoice Simple tiers cap monthly invoices, while Billdu tiers cap clients and team members.
  2. Users: a low-cost one-user plan does not fit a two-person shop. Unknown seat counts are not proof of fit.
  3. Billing interval: annual prices require a larger upfront commitment even when the table shows a monthly equivalent.
  4. Payment processing: transaction fees can exceed the subscription cost at sufficient payment volume. Tofu discloses a 1% platform fee in addition to Stripe; Jobber discloses 1% for ACH.
  5. Setup and migration: none of these public pricing pages gave me a verified setup or migration amount, so ask before assuming it is zero.

I build an invoicing app myself, as my about page explains, so keep that in mind as you read. The numbers matter more than my opinion. Run the 60-invoice example against your own volume and seat count before you trust a headline price.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free invoicing app for a small service business?

Zoho Invoice is free, but its current public pages do not state enough usage-limit detail to verify that a specific invoice volume or team size will fit. Check the limits shown inside the product before relying on it for a busy shop.

Does Jobber have a free trial?

Yes. Jobber's pricing page offers 14 days of full access to the Grow plan with no credit card required. It has no free plan after that.

How much does Tofu cost for a team?

Tofu's Team plan lists a standard price of $79 a month or $29 a month billed annually and covers up to five workers. Its pricing page also displays temporary promotional prices, which are not used in this comparison.

Does Billdu have a free plan?

No. Billdu's pricing page lists no free plan and offers a 7-day free trial with full access to every feature. The cheapest paid plan, Lite, costs $4.99 a month billed annually and includes unlimited invoices.
